Jura Connect brings your Jura coffee machine into Homey — as long as it has the WiFi Connect module fitted. Everything runs locally on your own network: no cloud, no Jura account, and no dependency on the internet being up.

Once paired, Homey can read your machine's live status (ready, heating up, needs water or beans, tray full) and brew your favourite coffee, espresso or cappuccino as part of a flow. Start your first cup automatically when your alarm goes off, or get a warning the moment the machine needs attention, without ever picking up your phone.

The app ships with product data for a wide range of Jura models, so pairing usually recognises your machine automatically.
